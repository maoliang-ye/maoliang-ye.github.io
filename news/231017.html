叶茂亮副教授在行为与组织经济学旗舰刊物JEBO发表关于团队激励的论文

2025-03-31

       近日，叶茂亮副教授及合作者Richard B. Freeman、潘晓菲、杨晓兰在行为与组织经济学旗舰刊物Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization发表论文；四位作者以姓氏字母排序（alphabetical order）、均列为共同第一作者，叶教授为通信作者。该文采用了真实努力实验（real-effort game），探究不同团队激励机制（包括：计件工资、平均分配、赢者通吃）如何提升团队中具有能力异质性的员工的努力水平和绩效。研究发现，平均分配方案通过提升低能力员工的努力水平而能够提高团队生产力。这一与传统理论结果相悖的实验结果可以被“愧疚厌恶”（guilt aversion）所解释；论文也讨论了愧疚厌恶及平均分配激励效果的可能适用范围。该研究采用实验方法并引入行为经济学视角，契合叶教授的主要研究领域；其具体应用话题则属于人事经济学（personnel economics），是组织经济学与劳动经济学结合的领域，也是叶教授核心的研究领域之一。在与本文密切相关的团队激励、分配方式、社会偏好等方面，叶教授及其课题组也有多年科研累积，并将在该话题进行持续深入研究。在行为经济学领域，叶教授已在PNAS, Management Science, Governance, Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization (x3), Journal of Comparative Economics, European Journal of Political Economy, 及 Social Indicators Research发表多篇论文。这些研究有着丰富的政策和管理应用启示，并与相关的应用微观经济学领域交叉（如公共政策与政治经济学，管理与组织经济学，劳动与发展经济学）。

      论文摘要：Despite a large literature on team incentives, studies on teams in a purely financial sense are limited. In such environments, team members independently engage in tasks with identifiable individual contributions, while their compensation is partially linked to team outputs. We conducted an experiment of such scenario with three distribution schemes (equal sharing, individual piece rate, and winner-takes-all) and examined these schemes both with and without a team threshold. Our results showed the surprising power of equal sharing on improving team productivity compared to winner-takes-all and individual piece-rate, contradicting the predictions of the standard economics theory. Our findings reveal that the higher team output observed under  equal sharing was driven by the increased productivity of less able workers. This could be attributed to an explanation of guilt aversion and cannot be illuminated by several alternative theories. We also found that participants preferred piece rate over the other schemes. Yet, the presence of a team threshold highlighted the importance of cooperation, leading to a greater preference for equal sharing. Our findings suggest that organizations with workers of varying abilities are likely to benefit from an appropriate equal sharing component tailored to their responsiveness to sharing in rewards.
