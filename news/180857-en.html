Maoliang Ye attends a workshop on charitable donations and public goods

2023-04-01

English translation of the original Chinese article.

At the invitation of the School of Economics at Shandong University, Associate Professor Maoliang Ye attended the Workshop on Charitable Giving and Public Goods Provision on April 1–2, 2023. Organized by Professor Jipeng Zhang, the workshop brought together Chinese researchers on charitable giving and voluntary public-goods provision. Their methods included laboratory, online and field experiments, as well as quantitative and econometric analysis of surveys and large datasets. The themes related closely to discussions of the third distribution and common prosperity.

Ye presented recent research online, titled “Peer Effects in Philanthropic Behaviors: Evidence from Random Assignment of Roommates.” The presentation drew dozens of scholars and students and prompted discussion and constructive comments. Ye also attended the other presentations and offered feedback.
