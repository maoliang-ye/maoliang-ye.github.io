Prof. Maoliang Ye Delivered a Talk at the 2002 International Meeting of the Association for Public Economic Theory

2022-06-18

Prof. Maoliang Ye attended its 2022 international conference of the Association for Public Economic Theory (APET) and delivered a talk titled  “Building Cooperation through Gradualism in Finitely Repeated Investment Games.”  APET is dedicated to the promotion of theoretical research in all areas of public finance and to the facilitation of communication amongst economists working in these areas. The meeting took place in France in a offline-online mixed mode from June 8 to June 10.
