Maoliang Ye presents at Shandong University’s behavioral and experimental economics seminar

2022-10-04

English translation of the original Chinese article.

At the invitation of Shandong University's Institute for Economic Research, Associate Professor Maoliang Ye presented “Team Incentives and Lower Ability Workers: An Experimental Study on Real-Effort Tasks” online on September 28, 2022, as part of its behavioral and experimental economics seminar series. The presentation attracted dozens of scholars and students from China and abroad, prompted discussion including contributions from Shuguang Jiang, and generated constructive suggestions.

The study is part of Ye's behavioral and experimental research on team incentives, compensation and human-resource management in labor, personnel and organizational economics. The latest version was released as an NBER working paper in September 2022.
