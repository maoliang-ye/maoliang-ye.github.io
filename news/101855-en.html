Maoliang Ye awarded a National Natural Science Foundation of China grant

2020-11-02

English translation of the original Chinese article.

In September 2020, Associate Professor Maoliang Ye received a National Natural Science Foundation of China general grant in experimental and behavioral economics (G0302). Since his doctoral dissertation, Ye has conducted research in experimental and behavioral economics and built academic connections in China and abroad. His publications include work in Management Science; his research on team cooperation and incentives combines theory and experiments. The review panel evaluated the proposal highly and approved it without revisions.
