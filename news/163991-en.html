Management Science paper on gradualism cited in the Journal of Economic Theory

2022-09-20

English translation of the original Chinese article.

Xiameng Hua and Joel Watson of the University of California, San Diego, published “Starting Small in Project Choice: a Discrete-Time Setting with a Continuum of Types” in the Journal of Economic Theory in 2022. Their paper discusses and cites “One Step at a Time: Does Gradualism Build Coordination?”, an experimental and behavioral theory study by Associate Professor Maoliang Ye and his coauthors published in Management Science. The citation reflects international attention to the work in economic theory.
