Maoliang Ye serves as discussant at a behavioral economics workshop at SWUFE

2022-09-26

English translation of the original Chinese article.

At the invitation of the China Center for Behavioral Economics and Finance at Southwestern University of Finance and Economics, Associate Professor Maoliang Ye served as a discussant at its workshop on the morning of September 25. He listened to research presentations by center researchers and provided comments. The invited discussants were well-known early-career behavioral and experimental economists from China and abroad.
