Maoliang Ye speaks at Shanghai Jiao Tong University’s young scholars salon

2020-11-02

English translation of the original Chinese article.

At the invitation of Professor Bangcheng Liu, associate dean of Shanghai Jiao Tong University's School of International and Public Affairs, Associate Professor Maoliang Ye gave an online seminar on October 28 entitled “Does Gradualism Build Cooperation? Evidence from A Finitely Repeated Investment Experiment.”

Ye discussed the importance and difficulty of cooperation in investor–investee, trustor–trustee and principal–agent relationships, and explained the experimental test and theoretical investigation of gradualism. After the talk, Ye, Liu and participants discussed the experimental results, theoretical interpretation, practical applications and the relationship between experimental economics and public administration, with interest in further discussion and cooperation.
