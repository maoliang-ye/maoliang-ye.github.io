Prof. Ye Gave A Talk at Wellbeing Research Centre of Oxford University

2023-05-10

        Invited by Prof.  Jan-Emmanuel De Neve, the director of the Wellbeing Research Centre at Oxford University，Prof. Maoliang Ye shared in a talk his research on using Chinese twins data to estimate the effect of income on happiness, one fundamental topic in economic, psychological, and social sciences.  (Update: This study has been published at PNAS in June).

        The Wellbeing Research Centre at Oxford University is the most important research center on happiness and well-being, and is dedicated to promote related academic and policy studies.
