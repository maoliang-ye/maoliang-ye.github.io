Tingting Ding presents research on school choice at SUSTech

2023-06-17

English translation of the original Chinese article.

On June 13, 2023, at the invitation of Associate Professor Maoliang Ye, Associate Professor Tingting Ding of Shanghai University of Finance and Economics gave an online seminar titled ‘Why Do Participants Make Dominated Choices in Strategy-proof School Choice Mechanisms?’ Ding received her economics PhD from New York University. Her research in experimental and behavioral economics has appeared in the Economic Journal and Games and Economic Behavior, and she has held NSFC grants.

Strategy-proofness is an important consideration in school choice: participants can submit their true preferences without studying others' preferences or strategies, reducing unfairness arising from differences in strategic sophistication. Yet field experiments and empirical evidence show that a substantial share of participants still choose dominated strategies. Ding and her coauthors use a laboratory experiment to investigate why.

The seminar considered inexperience, cognitive constraints, nonstandard utility and mistaken beliefs, and described an experiment based on deferred acceptance. When subjects had only one chance to participate, they were more likely to choose dominated strategies; expectations-based loss aversion and mistaken beliefs were major explanations. Repeated participation helped subjects learn to identify dominant strategies and reduced loss aversion. The findings inform policy interventions and welfare improvements in school choice. The 100-minute seminar attracted dozens of faculty and students and included an active discussion.
