Prof. Maoliang Ye Served Again as Reviewer for Public Administration Review

2023-02-27

In recent days, Public Administration Review (PAR), the top journal in the discipline of public administration, invited Prof. Maoliang Ye again to serve as an ad hoc reviewer.  The well-recognized top journals in public administration include Public Administration  Review (PAR), Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory (JPART), and Public Management Review (PMR), etc., which are focused on the administration of public organizations. The top general-interest journals in public policy include Governance, Policy Studies Journal (PSJ), Public Administration (PA), Journal of Policy Analysis and Management (JPAM), etc., which are focused on political/policy process or policy evaluation.
