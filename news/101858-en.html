Maoliang Ye gives a keynote at the annual game theory and experimental economics meeting

2020-11-02

English translation of the original Chinese article.

The 2020 annual conference on game theory and experimental economics, organized by the Game Theory and Experimental Economics Committee of the China Society of Quantitative Economics, was held online on October 31. Associate Professor Maoliang Ye gave an invited keynote, “Does Gradualism Build Cooperation? Evidence from A Finitely Repeated Investment Experiment.”

He discussed the importance and difficulty of cooperation in investor–investee, trustor–trustee and principal–agent relationships and explained the experimental test and theoretical investigation of gradualism.
