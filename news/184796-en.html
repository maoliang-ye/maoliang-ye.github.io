Maoliang Ye presents research at the Econometric Society African Meeting

2023-06-08

English translation of the original Chinese article.

The Econometric Society holds annual meetings across the world's regions. Its 2023 African Meeting took place in Kenya on June 1–3 in a hybrid format. Associate Professor Maoliang Ye participated online and presented “The Historical Origins of Economic Inequality: Evidence from the Slave Trade in Africa.” The paper examines the historical origins of economic inequality in Africa through the lens of the slave trade. Ye is the corresponding author.
