Maoliang Ye awarded another National Natural Science Foundation of China grant

2023-08-31

English translation of the original Chinese article.

In August 2023, Associate Professor Maoliang Ye received another National Natural Science Foundation of China general grant, titled ‘The Causal Impacts of Socioeconomic Status on Happiness: Evidence from Natural Experiments in China’, under the behavioral and experimental economics and population, labor and health economics classifications. Research on happiness has attracted increasing attention in economics, but existing studies face two limitations: determinants of happiness are endogenous, so correlations need not be causal, and research on Chinese well-being remains limited in both quantity and quality. The project aims to advance this field through causal inference in the Chinese context. Reviewers were generally positive and offered constructive suggestions; Ye plans to use their feedback in completing the research.

Happiness research spans behavioral, population and labor, development and public economics, as well as psychology, public policy and other social sciences. Ye's work on happiness and mental health has appeared in PNAS, Governance, Social Indicators Research and Applied Economics Letters, with further working papers and projects underway. He plans to continue exploring theory, statistical and econometric methods, causal analysis and policy interventions, and to deepen collaboration with scholars in China and abroad.

In 2020 Ye also received an NSFC general grant for theoretical and experimental research on teamwork incentives based on social preferences and bounded rationality. His work spans behavioral and experimental economics, labor economics, public policy, political economy, development and managerial economics.
