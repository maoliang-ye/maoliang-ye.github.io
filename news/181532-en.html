Shuhong Zhou delivers a research seminar at SUSTech

2023-04-13

English translation of the original Chinese article.

At the invitation of SUSTech's prospective Department of Applied Economics and Associate Professor Maoliang Ye, Professor Shuhong Zhou of Southwestern University of Finance and Economics delivered a seminar on ‘Attention Theory’ on April 10. Zhou directs the China Center for Behavioral Economics and Finance and is a fellow of the Econometric Society and the Society for the Advancement of Economic Theory. He has taught at the University of Arizona, Johns Hopkins University, UC Irvine and HKUST, and has published extensively in economics and other disciplines.

Drawing on cognitive science, the seminar considered the sources of cognitive limitations in decisions under risk and uncertainty. Attention theory distinguishes a top-down executive attention network from a bottom-up salience network. Together these can complement explanations of behavioral phenomena including Allais behavior, the fourfold pattern of risk attitudes and regret theory, with potential applications in finance, marketing and behavioral mechanism design.

Faculty and students from SUSTech and other Greater Bay Area institutions discussed the research in person, while dozens joined online. Afterward, more than ten in-person participants continued their discussion with Zhou over dinner.
