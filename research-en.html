Research

Research fields

Behavioral and experimental economics; applied microeconomics (organizational and managerial economics, public policy and political economy, labor, personnel and development studies).

The research connects behavioral economics with applied microeconomics and addresses public policy and corporate management.

Methods: experimental, empirical (e.g., natural experiments), or theoretical (mathematical).

Research topics

  • Social preferences; prosocial and organizational behavior; social norms; honesty, compliance and pandemic reporting
  • Team production and coordination; trust and investment cooperation; monitoring and feedback; compensation incentives; competition and cooperation
  • Inequality, distribution and redistribution; philanthropy; fairness; social welfare; government; fiscal preferences and public opinion
  • Subjective well-being, happiness, life and job satisfaction, mental health, health and health behavior
  • Local and rural governance, education, homeownership and anti-corruption
  • Related economic, social and political attitudes and psychology; the Chinese economy and institutions
