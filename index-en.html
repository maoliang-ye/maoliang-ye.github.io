About

Maoliang Ye joined the Business School of the Southern University of Science and Technology in 2020 as an associate professor. He got his Ph.D degree in Public Policy from Harvard University, under Professor Raj Chetty (Clark Medalist Winner, William A. Ackman Professor of Public Economics, Harvard Economics Department)，Professor Brigitte Madrian (currently Dean of the Marriott Business School at Brigham Young University), Professor Iris Bohnet (Albert Pratt Professor of Business and Government, Harvard Kennedy School Academic Dean), and Alberto Alesina (Nathaniel Ropes Professor of Political Economy，Harvard Economics Department, RIP). He served as a short-term consultant at the World Bank Group, and previously taught at Renmin University of China and Xiamen University. His main research areas center around behavioral economics and applied microeconomics as well as their applications in business management and public policy, and cover behavioral & experimental economics, managerial & organizational economics, labor & personnel economics, as well as public policy & political economy. His papers are published (or accepted) in renowned international journals in general science, management, economics, public policy, and social sciences, such as PNAS, Management Science, Journal of Accounting and Economics, Governance, Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization (x3), Journal of Comparative Economics (x2), European Journal of Political Economy, and Social Indicators Research. He is a Research Fellow of IZA and Global Labor Organization, and a member in councils of the China Association of Labor Economics, the China Labor Economist Forum, and the China Behavioral & Experimental Economics Forum. He has been a PI for two NSFC general grants and other major grants, serves as an ad hoc reviewer for NSFC grant applications and major journals in economics, management, public administration, political science, psychology, and other social sciences.
